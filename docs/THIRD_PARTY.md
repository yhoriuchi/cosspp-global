# Third-party materials

- Natural Earth country boundaries are public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ (accessed 2026-10-27). The official terms explicitly state that permission and author credit are not required (rechecked 2026-10-27). The homepage credit is therefore moved to About; Explore retains its source link. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson . Stored as `public/world.geojson` with property reduction and coordinate rounding; no imagery downloaded.
- React/React DOM, Vite, TypeScript, Zod, D3 Geo, tsx, Vitest, Playwright and axe-core are open-source dependencies; their precise versions and dependency graph are in `pnpm-lock.yaml`. Their package distributions include license notices. Retain applicable notices when distributing.
- Faculty facts and evidence are concise source-linked paraphrases; no full CVs, biographies, journal articles or photos are reproduced. The public site links to CVs; downloaded source documents used for review stay in ignored private caches and are not redistributed or relicensed.
- Official COSSPP logo: **not included in the website or build**, pending confirmation of permission for this particular site. On 2026-10-27 the official branding guide, university name/symbol policy and original SVG were checked; no Creative Commons license grant for the selected college lockup was found. Official resources: https://brand.fsu.edu/logos and https://policies.vpfa.fsu.edu/policies-and-procedures/general-university/use-university-name-and-identity-symbol . Academic lockups have institution-specific usage rules; access to a download does not establish a public reuse license. CC licenses do not license trademark rights: https://creativecommons.org/faq/#can-i-offer-material-under-a-cc-license-that-has-my-trademark-on-it-without-also-licensing-or-affecting-rights-in-the-trademark . Ask COSSPP Communications or University Marketing to confirm whether this directory is covered by authorized institutional use. No blanket legal conclusion about copyright eligibility is made. The unaltered source SVG is retained only in ignored private review storage.

No project-wide source/data license has been selected on the owner's behalf. Choose a suitable license and confirm data redistribution scope before public repository release.

The homepage and Explore geography share `src/components/WorldMap.tsx`, rendered with D3 Geo from public-domain Natural Earth geometry. The homepage is a uniformly colored illustration centered on Tallahassee; Explore geography uses the original Natural Earth projection and data-driven research-country links, without a Tallahassee marker. Both omit Antarctica. The former decorative SVG and generator remain only in ignored local design archives and are not included in the build. No map asset is an FSU/COSSPP logo.
