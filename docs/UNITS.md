# Unit classifications and review — 2026-10-27

“Unit” covers departments, the Askew School, academic programs, centers, institutes and a lab. University-wide/other-college affiliations are labeled separately; listing them does not make them COSSPP departments. This registry represents affiliations found in the directory, not a complete census of every college unit or employee. Integrated Studies is a program within ISS, not a separate department. A record's primary unit is not inferred from its source website, degree discipline or a center roster.

## Review decisions

- Reviewed all 176 existing primary-unit assignments and additional-affiliation evidence, against the current official college organization categories, named unit rosters and individual profiles. Source access is sometimes via indexed official content where direct retrieval times out; individual field evidence retains that distinction. This is not an HR appointment audit or a claim of complete roster coverage.
- Maria Cristina Ramos: official sources conflict in emphasis: the current Sociology roster/profile header gives Assistant Professor of Sociology, while biography text and the college update identify ISS teaching. Retained the current Sociology primary placement/rank and added the explicitly documented ISS affiliation; no inferred change of home or rank. Removed an unsupported source-evidence assertion that the college update described a fall-2026 tenure-track hire.
- Lonna Atkeson: added her explicitly documented directorship of the LeRoy Collins Institute; Political Science remains primary.
- Ziqi Li: independently corroborated existing SDSC membership on the center’s named faculty page and replaced the broad wildfire-news attribution for that field with this specific source. No membership change.
- Lisa Turner de Vera: added the explicitly documented Urban and Regional Planning affiliated-faculty role from the current college update; ISS remains primary.
- Integrated Studies and ISS remain distinct program choices because both official program lists identify them; the registry records ISS as Integrated Studies' parent.
- Keep James L. Gibson Political Science only as explicitly directed by the owner. Keep Unknown primary placement for Densu/Turner; their verified AAS affiliation provides membership without inventing a home appointment.
- Use one shared membership function for filtering and Units counts: primary plus documented additional affiliations, deduplicated within a person. Counts show current regular/teaching faculty and may overlap across units. Hide zero-default-count cards; the dropdown retains verified units with additional listings for use with the appointment toggle. No arbitrary first-affiliation fallback or falsely exclusive partition.
- Preserve `primary_department`, the `department` URL parameter and the `departments` route as stable compatibility keys; public labels use Unit/Units. Newly encountered unit names must be classified in the reviewed registry before publication. Do not reintroduce an outside university as a college unit.
